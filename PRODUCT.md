# Loci Loom

**Promise:** Turn anything you study into a world you can walk through.

Loci Loom is a mobile learning game that converts a topic into a short sequence of vivid locations, characters, and retrieval challenges. Instead of rereading notes, a learner revisits a visual story, recalls the concept attached to each scene, and strengthens the trail through spaced practice.

## Why this can win

- **Clear student problem:** passive rereading feels productive but produces weak recall.
- **Immediate demo:** enter “cellular respiration,” watch five memorable scenes appear, then complete a recall run.
- **Designed product:** cinematic scenes, tactile progress, strong hierarchy, accessible motion, and an offline-first loop.
- **Natural monetization:** the bundled starter trail is free, and learners can create three more; Scholar Pass unlocks unlimited trails and deeper adaptive practice.
- **Credible expansion:** Nemotron can generate and critique trails; Alexa+ can conduct hands-free daily recall while preserving progress.

## Shipped web-first scope

The shipped MVP includes curated trails for the immune system, SQL joins, and French verbs; source-grounded or arbitrary-topic model generation; a fixed five-landmark palace map; five-scene playback; three retrieval angles; accuracy-based review scheduling; validated persistence; a library; and a RevenueCat-backed entitlement boundary. It does not pretend that an unconfigured store is live: the web preview labels its interactive demo entitlement, while native builds use the production billing adapter.

## Prize strategy

1. **RevenueCat Next Gen — primary.** Best balance of prize value, student eligibility, no store fee, and a repo/video-based judging path.
2. **Nebius Best Apps and Agents / Personal AI — extension.** Nemotron is the preferred live generator when a Nebius key is configured; persisted adaptive learning is already connected.
3. **Amazon Alexa+ — extension.** A stateful daily-recall Agent Skill is credible, but device/simulation requirements make it a worse first 24-hour target.
